# UK Mobile Telecom Revenue Analytics

## Project Title
**Evaluating the Drivers of Mobile Telecommunications Revenue in the UK: A Business Analytics Study**

## Project Overview
This repository supports an MSc Business Analytics research project examining the factors associated with mobile telecommunications retail revenue in the United Kingdom.

The study uses secondary telecommunications market data published by Ofcom and applies quantitative business analytics techniques using Python.

## Research Question
**To what extent are changes in mobile subscriptions, service usage and customer mix associated with mobile telecommunications retail revenue in the UK?**

## Research Objectives
1. Analyse historical trends in UK mobile telecommunications revenue, subscriptions, mobile data usage, voice and messaging activity, and customer mix.
2. Examine the statistical relationships between mobile retail revenue and selected market and usage variables.
3. Develop statistical models to evaluate the factors associated with changes in UK mobile telecommunications retail revenue and interpret their business implications.

## Data Source
The principal dataset is the **Ofcom Telecommunications Market Data Update Q1 2026**, published by the Office of Communications (Ofcom), United Kingdom.

The dataset provides historical telecommunications market statistics covering variables such as:
- Mobile retail revenue
- Mobile subscriptions
- Post-pay and pre-pay subscriptions
- Mobile data usage
- Mobile voice usage
- SMS/MMS volumes
- Mobile broadband subscriptions
- Average monthly retail revenue per subscriber
